Our Mission

BehindTheMath exists to make mathematical learning engaging, visual, and accessible. Most math resources show you a formula and expect you to trust it. We believe every learner deserves to understand why a formula works, what its variables mean, when it applies, and how to use it — both by hand and in code.

Every lesson on this site answers four questions:

  1. What does the formula say?
  2. Why does it work?
  3. What changes when its variables change?
  4. How can I apply it in a real problem or program?

The experience aims to combine the clarity of a well-edited textbook, the feedback of an interactive lab, and the speed of a modern developer reference.

Core Principles

  • 🧠
    Understanding before memorization We explain symbols, assumptions, units, and intuition before presenting a formula as a fact to memorize.
  • 🎮
    Learn by changing values Sliders, number inputs, step controls, and animated diagrams make the relationship visible.
  • 📖
    Progressive disclosure Begin with a simple explanation and reveal derivations, edge cases, and advanced notes on request.
  • 💻
    Code is part of the lesson C (C11) and Python 3 examples side by side, with comments, sample output, and edge-case notes.
  • ♿
    Accessible by default Keyboard navigation, sufficient contrast, reduced-motion support, semantic HTML, and readable mathematical notation.
  • 🏗️
    Honest static-first architecture Core lessons work without a server. No account required. Progress is stored locally when used.

Who is this for?

  • Secondary and university students building mathematical foundations
  • Engineering and CSE students connecting formulas to practical problems
  • Competitive programmers learning number theory, combinatorics, and complexity
  • Data Science and AI/ML learners reviewing algebra, vectors, probability, and calculus
  • Curious learners who want to understand the mathematics behind everyday phenomena

The site supports beginner, intermediate, and advanced material. No prior level is assumed across all topics.

Accessibility

BehindTheMath targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a design goal. Key commitments include:

Keyboard navigationAll interactive controls are keyboard-accessible, including tabs, sliders, and modals.
Focus indicatorsVisible focus rings in orange (#F4A261) on all focusable elements.
Reduced motionAll animations respect prefers-reduced-motion: reduce. Labs update instantly without animation.
Color contrastText colors are chosen to meet AA contrast ratios on all backgrounds.
Semantic HTMLProper landmark regions: header, nav, main, section, footer with ARIA labels.
Screen readersMeaningful alt text, aria-live regions for dynamic content, aria-label on interactive controls.
Math renderingKaTeX renders formulas with accessible MathML structure where possible.

Content Approach

Lesson content is authored to be mathematically accurate, with careful attention to:

  • Conditions and domain restrictions stated alongside each formula
  • Unit conventions made explicit
  • Common mistakes and edge cases highlighted
  • Code examples tested for correctness and edge-case handling
No accreditation claim BehindTheMath is an independent educational resource. It does not claim certification, formal accreditation, or endorsement by any educational institution.

What is and isn't included

Included: Formula lessons, interactive visualizations, C and Python code examples, practice questions, a searchable formula library, and a visual lab.

Not included (by design): User accounts, cloud-synced progress, server-side databases, arbitrary code execution, paid courses, or social features. These may be considered later as separate features.
