Learn Mathematics

Structured lessons from foundations to advanced topics — with interactive labs and code examples at every stage.

Recommended Learning Path

Study in this order for the best foundation

Foundations → Exponents & Algebra → Geometry & Trigonometry → Counting & Probability → Statistics & Linear Algebra → Calculus → Physics → CSE Applications
🔢

Foundations

Arithmetic, fractions, percentages, ratios, exponents, logarithms, sequences

  • Percentages & Ratios
    P = (Part/Whole) × 100 — proportional reasoning
    Beginner
  • Exponents & Logarithms
    log_b(x) = y ⟺ b^y = x — inverse operations
    Intermediate
🔑

Number Theory & Programming

GCD, LCM, primes, modular arithmetic, binary numbers, bit manipulation

  • GCD & LCM
    Euclidean algorithm — gcd(a,b)·lcm(a,b) = a·b
    Beginner
⚖️

Algebra

Identities, linear and quadratic equations, complex numbers

  • Quadratic Formula
    x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac)) / 2a — roots of ax²+bx+c=0
    Intermediate⚡ Lab
📐

Geometry

2D shapes, area, perimeter, 3D shapes, coordinate geometry

  • Circle Area & Circumference
    A = πr², C = 2πr — why area grows as r²
    Beginner⚡ Lab
  • Pythagorean Theorem
    a² + b² = c² — right triangle side relationship
    Beginner⚡ Lab
📏

Trigonometry

Ratios, unit circle, identities, compound angles, triangle laws

  • Trigonometric Unit Circle
    sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 — angles to coordinates
    Intermediate⚡ Lab
🎲

Counting & Probability

Factorials, permutations, combinations, probability, conditional probability

  • Permutations & Combinations
    P(n,r) = n!/(n−r)! · C(n,r) = n!/r!(n−r)!
    Intermediate
  • Probability & Bayes' Theorem
    P(A|B) = P(B|A)P(A)/P(B) — updating with evidence
    Intermediate
📊

Statistics

Mean, variance, standard deviation, z-score, data analysis

  • Mean & Standard Deviation
    σ = √(Σ(xi−μ)²/N) — measuring spread around the mean
    Beginner
🔲

Linear Algebra & Vectors

Vectors, dot & cross products, projections, matrices, determinants, eigenvalues, SVD

  • Dot Product & Projections
    u·v = |u||v|cos θ · proj_v u = ((u·v)/|v|²)v — orthogonality & projections
    Intermediate
  • Cross Product & Normal Vectors
    u × v = |u||v|sin θ n̂ — 3D perpendicular vectors & parallelogram area
    Intermediate
  • Matrix Transformations & Determinants
    C = AB · det(A) = ad − bc · A⁻¹ = (1/det(A)) adj(A) — linear systems
    Intermediate
  • Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors & SVD
    Av = λv · det(A − λI) = 0 · A = U Σ Vᵀ — spectral analysis & machine learning
    Advanced

Calculus

Limits, derivatives, integrals, Taylor and Maclaurin series

  • Derivatives & Tangent Slope
    f'(x) = lim(h→0)(f(x+h)−f(x))/h — instantaneous rate of change
    Advanced⚡ Lab
